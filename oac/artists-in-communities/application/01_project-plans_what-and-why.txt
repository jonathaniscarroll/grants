PROJECT PLANS: What are you planning to do, and what do you want to achieve with this project? [Maximum 425 words]

STATUS: DRAFT 4 (about 395 words by estimate). Sally and Robin led the Forest Courtyard and Toronto Waldorf School productions together.

--- DRAFT ---

Over four months (November 2026 to March 2027), I will plan the community co-creation of The Seer's Prophecy (working title), an original folk opera drawing on Norse mythology and centred on the stories of its female characters. This planning phase prepares a later project in which students at Forest Courtyard Initiative, a Waldorf-inspired forest and farm school near Tottenham, and residents of Hesperus Fellowship, an independent-living retirement community in Thornhill, co-create the work with me through devised theatre, songwriting circles, crafting, creative movement and other workshops.

The opera is at an early stage of conceptualization. Before anyone is asked to join, I want to build the foundation for shared authorship. I will develop a storyboard, flesh out the characters, and write three to five initial songs to establish the work's sound. These materials will be offered to both communities as invitations to respond to, not finished products.

Sally Lawrence, an artist and educator rooted in both communities, will mentor me and act as liaison between me and the two organizations. She will build a curriculum that structures how participants will take part and establish visual motifs for the collaborative work. Sally and I will meet every two weeks, and we will hold monthly meetings with stakeholders at the school and the retirement community, so that both partners help shape the plan and are invested in it before the next phase begins.

This builds on my earlier work. A Song for Hephaestus, my folk opera, uses Greek mythology to explore disability, injustice and the liberatory potential of creative labour. It was developed in community and premiered at the Chester Playhouse in 2024. Sally and I then led youth adaptations of it with children at Forest Courtyard Initiative (May 2025) and at the Toronto Waldorf School summer camp (July 2025). In June 2026 we created Children of Abraham, a review show with original songs and script about four Abrahamic faiths (Judaism, Christianity, Islam and the Baha'i Faith), with children at Forest Courtyard.

By March 2027, I want to have:
- a storyboard, character outlines and three to five initial songs
- a participation curriculum and visual motifs
- strong relationships and shared expectations with both partners
- a clear picture of each community's access needs
- a realistic design for an intergenerational project where children and elders have equal input into new work
