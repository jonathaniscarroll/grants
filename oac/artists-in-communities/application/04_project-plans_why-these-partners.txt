--- DRAFT ---

I have worked with both Forest Courtyard Initiative and Hesperus Fellowship over time and have built lasting, positive relationships with each. Both communities want growth, learning and connection, and both have room for exploration and creativity.

Forest Courtyard Initiative offers holistic, nature-based, arts-integrated education, and my work in mythology and folklore fits well within its curriculum. Sally Lawrence is lead teacher for its grade 2 to 7 children. She is an artist, educator and community leader, and she will act as liaison between me and both organizations.

Hesperus is a creative hub, home to many retired artists, educators and thinkers from around the world, with a long history of upholding the arts. I have done care work, performed at monthly soirees and worked on plays with residents, and I have much to learn from them. As a second partner, Hesperus can help lead the project in directions I could not find alone.

How we will work together: during this phase I will meet monthly with stakeholders at both organizations, and Sally and I will meet every two weeks. I will test ideas for songwriting circles, crafting nights, skill-sharing and devising workshops. Students at the school will explore Norse mythology in their curriculum, so they come to the project already curious. Sally will share my storyboard and songs with the children as they develop, and invite interested Hesperus residents to say how they would like to lead or learn.

What partners will contribute:
- Forest Courtyard Initiative: space for events and workshops, volunteers providing snacks, participants, and outreach to families
- Hesperus Fellowship: space, volunteers, residents' skills and guidance, and outreach to its community
- Sally Lawrence: mentorship, curriculum design, and liaison between all parties
