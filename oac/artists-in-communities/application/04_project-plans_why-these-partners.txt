--- DRAFT ---

I have worked with both Forest Courtyard Initiative and Hesperus Fellowship over time and have built lasting, positive relationships with each. Both communities want growth, learning and connection, and both have room for exploration and creativity.

Forest Courtyard Initiative offers holistic, nature-based, arts-integrated education, and my work in mythology and folklore fits well within its curriculum. Sally Lawrence is lead teacher for its grade 2 to 7 children. She is an artist, educator and community leader, and she will act as liaison between me and both organizations.

Hesperus is a creative hub, home to many retired artists, educators and thinkers from around the world, with a long history of upholding the arts. I have done care work, performed at monthly soirees and worked on plays with residents, and I have much to learn from them. As a second partner, Hesperus can help lead the project in directions I could not find alone.

How we will work together: during this phase I will meet monthly with stakeholders at both organizations, and Sally and I will meet every two weeks. I will plan songwriting circles, crafting nights, skill-sharing and devising workshops with their input. Students at the school will explore Norse mythology in their curriculum, so they come to the project already curious. Sally will share my storyboard and songs with the children as they develop, and invite interested Hesperus residents to say how they would like to lead or learn.

What partners will contribute: this planning phase asks little of our partners beyond their time and counsel. They will take part in monthly meetings, tell us about their communities' needs and hopes, and help shape the plan. Both organizations have given signed letters of intent. They are not being asked to provide anything further until a follow-on project is funded.

If that project goes ahead, we expect Forest Courtyard Initiative to offer space, volunteers for snacks, participants and outreach to families, and Hesperus Fellowship to offer space, volunteers, residents' skills and guidance, and outreach. Those commitments would be confirmed in letters for that application.

Sally Lawrence will contribute mentorship, curriculum design, and liaison between all parties.
