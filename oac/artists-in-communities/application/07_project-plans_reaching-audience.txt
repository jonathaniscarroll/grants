Outreach will run through Sally Lawrence and our two partners, who can bring these stories to people already interested in them. Forest Courtyard Initiative, Hesperus and Sally also have connections to other schools and groups in the area, which will matter for larger events later.

The planning phase gives us time to build excitement and investment before any formal creation begins.

At Forest Courtyard, Sally will introduce Norse mythology in her classroom and share the pitch deck, storyboard and song drafts as they develop, so children are in conversation with the piece early and have time to form their own ideas.

At Hesperus, over these four to five months, Sally will help me find residents who want to lead or learn: songwriters who could lead song circles, writers who could guide lyric and wordplay workshops, and visual artists who could shape crafting and world-building. Others may simply want to learn a new skill.

Planned workshops will cover music creation, lyric writing, devised theatre, character work, and designing props and set pieces. Events will be geared to different groups, including intergenerational sessions, so that each participant has a way in.
