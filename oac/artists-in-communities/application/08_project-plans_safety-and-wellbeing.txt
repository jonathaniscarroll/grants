PROJECT PLANS: Describe how the safety and well-being (physical, emotional, social, or other) of all involved in the project is addressed in your project plan. [Maximum 250 words]

STATUS: DRAFT 2 (about 225 words). Partner named Hesperus Village; session wording made conditional to match section 14, which budgets no participant sessions in this phase. Original point-form notes are in git history.

--- DRAFT ---

Safety and accessibility are central to this planning phase. With Forest Courtyard Initiative and Hesperus Village, we will keep a living document of participants' needs and who wants to take part, so every space can be made accessible.

Physical: we will check each venue for step-free access, wheelchair space, and seating close to the action for people with vision or hearing impairments. Any sessions will include extra time for breaks, rest and water, with Forest Courtyard assistant teachers checking in. Public health protocols will apply: stay home if sick, hand sanitizing, and good air circulation.

Legal: we will confirm chaperone and staff ratios, vulnerable-sector or criminal record checks for school staff and facilitators, and first-aid-trained staff on site.

Emotional and social: the project is designed to reduce isolation by bringing people together. Collective creation flattens hierarchy and treats every voice equally. Students will take part in a short intergenerational orientation on working with elders, and elders on working with children.

Participants will have a designated person to go to, confidentially, if they feel unsafe or have additional needs. Story-gathering will have clear boundaries: no personal stories are used in the work, and anything shared is combined and anonymous. Participants may always pass or sit out. A code of conduct covering touch, personal space and language will be set before we begin and revisited in check-ins.
