This budget supports a four-month planning phase, November 11, 2026 to March 11, 2027, for the community co-creation of The Seer's Prophecy.

Artist fee ($6,800): Robin Moir will work about 10 hours per week for 17 weeks, or 170 hours, at $40 per hour. This time covers developing the storyboard and characters, writing three to five initial songs, preparing materials to share with both partner communities, inviting stakeholders at Forest Courtyard Initiative and Hesperus Village to monthly meetings, planning accessible spaces and sessions, and preparing a plan for the follow-on project.

Mentor fee ($3,000): Sally Lawrence will mentor Robin and act as liaison between Robin and both partner organizations. Her work includes biweekly mentoring and planning meetings with Robin, designing a curriculum to structure participation in the follow-on project, establishing visual motifs for the collaborative work, and attending the monthly stakeholder meetings to which both partners are invited. Robin is a professional artist who identifies as disabled and lives outside Toronto, both OAC priority groups, and this fee is requested from the program's mentorship funds.

Forest Courtyard Initiative and Hesperus Village have given letters of intent. They are not paid and are not being asked to provide space, volunteers or other contributions until a follow-on project is funded, so no in-kind or partner revenue is included here.

The budget has no administrative, marketing, venue or materials costs, because this phase is planning work.
