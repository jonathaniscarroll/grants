--- DRAFT ---

This project brings together two communities that rarely meet: rural children and their families at Forest Courtyard Initiative, near Tottenham, and retired artists, educators and thinkers at Hesperus Fellowship in Thornhill, many from around the world, and their families.

In this planning phase we will work mainly with the stakeholders of both organizations: school staff and leaders, Hesperus staff and interested residents. They will help shape what the later co-creation project looks like. Sally Lawrence will share the work in progress with the Forest Courtyard students in their classroom, and invite interested Hesperus residents to say how they would like to take part. In the follow-on project, children, elders and their families would be participants and co-creators.

The artists are Robin Moir, as lead artist, and Sally Lawrence, as mentor and liaison.
