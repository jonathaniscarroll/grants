# OAC Artists in Communities and Schools Projects: The Seer's Prophecy

Working folder for an Ontario Arts Council application (Planning category draft) for the community-engaged planning phase of *The Seer's Prophecy* (working title), an original folk opera drawing on Norse mythology.

- Program page: https://www.arts.on.ca/grants/artists-in-communities-and-schools-projects
- Program contact: Madeline Legg, Program Administrator, mlegg@arts.on.ca, 416-969-7401
- Deadline: October 15, 2026, 1 p.m. ET (next: check OAC page)
- Results: about 4.5 months after the deadline

## The people

- Robin Moir: lead artist, writing the piece
- Sally Lawrence: mentor, coordinator and liaison to the partner organizations
- Partners: Forest Courtyard Initiative (Tottenham, ON) and Hesperus Fellowship (Thornhill, ON)
- Jonathan Carroll is an outside advisor, not an applicant or artist
- Robert Moir is a separate person from Robin Moir

## Program rules that matter most

- Planning grant maximum is $7,500; Project is $12,500; Two-year Project is $20,000. Up to $3,000 more for mentorship in any category.
- Must include a confirmed non-arts community partner. Signed letters are mandatory; Planning applicants in early stages may submit letters of intent instead.
- Process over product: performances or presentations cannot be the primary goal or outcome.
- Professional artists must be paid. Applicants must be Ontario residents.
- Activity cannot start before the deadline, cannot finish before results, and must finish within two years of results. No public launch before results.
- Ineligible: artists employed by a school involved in the project, full or part time, during the project.
- Budget must balance at $0.

## Open flags before submitting

1. **Sally's employment.** Sally works full-time as Lead Grades teacher at Forest Courtyard Initiative, a partner school, and is listed as paid mentor/coordinator. OAC lists artists employed by a school involved in the project as ineligible applicants. Confirm with the program administrator how this applies to her paid role.
2. **Planning budget cap.** Robin at 10 hrs/week for 16 to 18 weeks at $40/hr is $6,400 to $7,200, which leaves little or nothing under the $7,500 cap for Sally's fee or other costs.
3. **Performances in the plan.** The notes mention two community preview performances and sharing soirees. Planning-phase activities should not look like performance is the main outcome.
4. **Over word limits.** Sections 04, 06, 07, 08 and 09 are flagged as over or incomplete in the notes.
5. **Partner naming.** Notes say Hesperus Fellowship in some places and Hesperus Village in others. Use the legal name consistently, matching the letter.
6. **Residency.** Confirm Ontario residency (12 months) for each paid artist and who the applicant is in Nova.
7. **Letters.** Signed letters (confirmation or intent) from both partners are required.
