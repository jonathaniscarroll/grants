# Jonathan Carroll

Interdisciplinary artist and creative technologist · Halifax, Nova Scotia
jonathancarroll@nscad.ca · https://jonathaniscarroll.github.io/cv/

## Education

2025–2027 — Master of Fine Arts (Studio), NSCAD University, Halifax, NS (in progress). Thesis research-creation: The Land Speaks; geolocated narrative, industrial history and environmental health in Cape Breton.

2011–2016 — Bachelor of Fine Arts (Integrated Media), OCAD University, Toronto, ON.

## Selected projects

2025–2026 — The Land Speaks. Geolocated browser-based augmented reality narrative addressing industrial pollution and autoimmune illness in Sydney, Nova Scotia.

2025–2026 — Spatial Narrative. Location-aware interactive fiction system using GPS, compass and dwell-triggered passages, with a reader and authoring tool.

2025–2026 — The Wrong John Carroll, cemetery archive and pallet altar project; Sourdough Synth, bioelectric signals from sourdough translated into solar-powered sound.

2019–present — VRIM (Virtual Reality Interactive Music). Participatory sound composition system sampling audiences and environments into generative sequences. Performed at the Art Gallery of Regina (2022); developed at Alchemy, Gibraltar Point Arts Centre (2025).

2023 — GAN of Living Skies. Solar-powered AI landscape painting machine, Art Gallery of Regina.

2022 — Hypercity. GPS-based augmented reality exhibition across Toronto featuring six artists; Long Winter Festival.

2018 — Resourced, with Ben McCarthy. Virtual reality documentary combining frontline workers’ self-narrated accounts with interactive metaphors, scanned figures and spatial sound; Mayworks Festival, Trinity Square Video.
