# Nova Scotia Arts — Grants to Organizations and Groups: Blue Moon

Working folder for an Arts Nova Scotia **Grants to Organizations and Groups** application, due **October 15, 2026**.

- Program page: https://artsns.ca/programs/grants-organizations-and-groups
- Online application form: https://artsns.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/OrgAppForm_PREVIEW_0.pdf
- Application checklist: https://artsns.ca/sites/default/files/2024-02/OSG_ApplicationChecklist.pdf
- Source documents: `ns arts - grants for orgs- oct 15 2026 - blue moon/`
- Results: 10–12 weeks after the deadline

## Online form fields

Complete and submit the online application form once. Key fields include:

- Organization or group name, address, contact, and registration information
- Whether the applicant is a non-profit, Nova Scotia-based publisher, or ad hoc group
- Operating-assistance status and annual amount, if applicable
- Application contact and chair/organizer details
- Generative-AI use disclosures
- Voluntary self-identification form
- Artistic discipline
- Grant category, project name, start and end dates, amount requested, and total expenses
- Brief two- to three-sentence project description

## Funding categories

| Category | Maximum per deadline |
|---|---:|
| Create and Present | $20,000 |
| Commission | $7,500 |
| Professional Development | $7,500 |

One application per category per deadline. Projects must begin after the application deadline.

## Submission rules

- Submit the online form once.
- Email remaining documents to `artsnssubmissions@novascotia.ca` by 11:59 p.m. on the deadline.
- Use subject line: `Your Name – Program Name – Category`.
- Number files in the intended opening order and include your name on every file.
- Keep attachments under 25 MB.
- Include audio and video as streaming links, not attachments.
- Do not use cloud-storage links, file-transfer services, or URL shorteners.
- Send one application per email.

## Assessment

Peer assessment committees assess:

1. Quality of the work, as evidenced by support material
2. Artistic merit of the project
3. Ability to carry out the project

## Open questions

1. Confirm applicant type: organization, publisher, or ad hoc group.
2. Confirm artistic discipline and funding category.
3. Confirm project name, dates, amount requested, and total expenses.
4. Confirm whether the project engages with cultures outside the applicant’s own.
5. Identify all key artists requiring CVs.
6. Select support material and prepare annotations.
7. Identify any required letters of intent, confirmation, invitation, or support.
