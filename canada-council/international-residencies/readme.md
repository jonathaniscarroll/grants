**Status:** In progress

**Program:** https://www.arts-services.ca/s/fundingopportunity/119JQ000003Vz5fYAC/6605?language=en_US

## Program snapshot

- Part of *Arts Across Canada and Abroad*; funds a residency abroad of **at least 5 consecutive days** to which you were **invited or selected**.
- Up to **$20,000** per application; max **2 applications per calendar year**.
- Ineligible: hosting a residency; activities in Canada.
- Assessed by peer committee: Relevance 50% (min 35/50), Impact 30% (min 15/30), Feasibility 20% (min 10/20); RECAP must be satisfied.
