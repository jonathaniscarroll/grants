## Program snapshot

- Part of *Arts Across Canada and Abroad*; funds a residency abroad of **at least 5 consecutive days** to which you were **invited or selected**.
- Up to **$20,000** per application; max **2 applications per calendar year**.
- Ineligible: hosting a residency; activities in Canada.
- Peer assessment: Relevance 50% (min 35/50), Impact 30% (min 15/30), Feasibility 20% (min 10/20); RECAP must be satisfied.

## Key facts so far

- 8 collective members plan to travel, now over **reading week in November 2026** (per Oct 13 email to NSCAD).
- NSCAD's Off-Campus Experiential Learning Fund offered up to **$16,000 for flights** (Sept 29, 2026), from a non-coursework fund. Declare it as other revenue; do not request Canada Council money for the same flights.
- NSCAD conditions: valid passports/visas, medical/travel insurance, travel waiver and OSE briefing, presentation and written report on return, names and project details released to the donor.
- Studio options at ZK/U: Studio 5 (44 sqm) or Studio 14 (75 sqm, three rooms, suited to collectives). Price not yet in repo.
