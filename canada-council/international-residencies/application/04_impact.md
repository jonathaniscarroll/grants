## 1. International profile and practice (650 characters max)

Presenting our research-creation practice to the international ZK/U community, and selling printed work at the December market, introduces Dada Issues and its members to artists, publishers and audiences outside Canada. Working in Berlin lets each member develop their own publication while testing our collective, affordable, artist-led model among international peers.

## 2. Connections, exchange and knowledge (850 characters max)

ZK/U is built on peer-to-peer learning. In shared studios, kitchens and project spaces we will exchange methods with other fellows, learn how independent presses and artist-run spaces operate in Berlin, and share our approach to low-cost, limited-run publishing. Two residency periods (November and December) let us build relationships in the first visit and deepen them through the market and presentation in the second.

## 3. Future opportunities (850 characters max)

The residency should lead to new publications and editions, distribution and trade contacts, invitations to book fairs and exchanges, and future collaborations with ZK/U fellows. It also gives members an international exhibition and sales record, a stronger base for later grant applications, and a model the collective can repeat with other host organizations.
