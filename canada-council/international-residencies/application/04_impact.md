## 1. International profile and practice (650 characters max, draft about 440)

Presenting our research and creation practices to the international ZK/U community, and selling printed work at the December market, introduces Dada Issues and its members to artists, publishers and audiences outside Canada. Each member develops their own publication in Berlin, a city with a deep independent-press and art-book culture, while we test our collective, affordable, artist-led model among international peers.

## 2. Connections, exchange and knowledge (850 characters max, draft about 474)

ZK/U is built on peer-to-peer learning. In shared studios, the kitchen and project spaces we will exchange methods with other fellows, learn how independent presses and artist-run spaces work in Berlin, and share our approach to low-cost, limited-run publishing. In November, three members scout print shops and paper-making resources and report back to the five December travellers. The second stay deepens these relationships through our group presentation and the market.

## 3. Future opportunities (850 characters max, draft about 525)

The residency should lead to new publications and editions, distribution and trade contacts, invitations to book fairs and exchanges, and future collaborations with ZK/U fellows. Our experiments combining block printing, paper making, hand-binding, image transfers and augmented reality can become repeatable, affordable formats for future editions. As members prepare for careers after the MFA, an international presentation and sales record and the contacts made here support later grant applications and hosts beyond ZK/U.
