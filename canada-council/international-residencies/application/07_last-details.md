## Draft

Dada Issues Collective Press is based in Kjipuktuk (Halifax), Mi'kma'ki, and is run by student artists. We separately received up to $16,000 from NSCAD's Off-Campus Experiential Learning Fund, a non-coursework travel fund, to cover airfare for eight members. That funding covers flights only. This request covers the residency fee and production materials so the collective can work in Berlin for two periods. NSCAD requires each traveller to hold insurance, sign a travel waiver, and give a presentation and written report after the trip. Our practice is shaped by publishing affordable, limited-run books, so access to materials and studio space is the main barrier to doing this work abroad.
