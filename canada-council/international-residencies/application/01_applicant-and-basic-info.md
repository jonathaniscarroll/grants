## About the applicant (source: dadaissues.ca/information)

Dada Issues [collective press] is an art collective and independent publishing house in Kjipuktuk (Halifax), Nova Scotia. It makes affordable, limited-run art books and 1/1 art objects: artist books, experimental fiction, multiples, ephemera, pamphlets and propaganda. Its work is rooted in art, critical theory and experimental poetics, from queer, feminist, environmentalist, anti-capitalist and anti-colonialist perspectives. It was founded to address artists bearing production costs and the rising cost of physical books, and to build community around physical art books.
