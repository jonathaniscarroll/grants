Dada Issues Collective Press is an art collective and independent publishing house in Kjipuktuk (Halifax), Nova Scotia. We make affordable, limited-run artist books, experimental fiction, multiples, ephemera and pamphlets. We began because artists often carry the cost of producing their own work, and because physical books keep getting more expensive. Our collective of eight members will take part in an artist-directed residency at ZK/U – Center for Art and Urbanistics in Berlin, in two periods: November 7–14, 2026 and December 10–18, 2026.

During the residency each member will develop work for their own publication project, working side by side as a collective in the studio space provided by ZK/U. We are applying for the creative research and creation, and the networking it enables. In November we will research, draft and make prototypes; in December we will produce and print the finished matter [specify processes, e.g. risograph, screenprint, binding] and share it publicly.

Our press works the same way: artist-led, community-oriented and driven by materiality and care. Residents contribute to communal life, from weekly shared dinners to events in the project spaces, which suits a collective that treats publishing as a social practice.
