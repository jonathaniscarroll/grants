## Host facts available for the draft

- ZK/U was founded in 2012 in the Moabit city garden, in a former freight yard, as an artist-initiated venue with a residency program, project spaces and neighbourhood spaces.
- It describes itself as a hub for exchange between global discourses and local practice, with collaborative practice and peer-to-peer learning at its core.
- Fellows are encouraged to contribute to communal life through actions, collectives or maintaining the space.
- Event spaces on two levels and a roof terrace host screenings, workshops, concerts and conferences; the shared kitchen hosts a weekly Monday Dinner.
- Studio 5: 44 sqm, ground floor, separate sleeping area, private bathroom, direct access to kitchen and terrace, utility sink, lofted area.
- Studio 14: 75 sqm, upper floor, three rooms, kitchen and bathroom, suited to collectives.
