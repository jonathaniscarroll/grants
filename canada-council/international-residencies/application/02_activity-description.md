## Draft

Dada Issues Collective Press is an art collective and independent publishing house in Kjipuktuk (Halifax), Nova Scotia. We make affordable, limited-run artist books, experimental fiction, multiples, ephemera and pamphlets. We began because artists often carry the cost of producing their own work, and because physical books keep getting more expensive. We have been invited to ZK/U – Center for Art and Urbanistics in Berlin for two residency periods: November 7–14, 2026, for three members, and December 10–18, 2026, for five members.

At ZK/U each member will develop work for their own publication project, using the studio space provided and working side by side as a collective. In November the first three members will begin producing work and scout printing options in Berlin. In December five members will continue producing, finish editions, and take part in the ZK/U market, where we can sell the printed matter we make. We will also present our research and creation practices to the international community at ZK/U.

We will explore printing in Berlin and work with a range of methods suited to our individual practices: block printing, paper making, hand-bound books, image transfers and augmented reality. Combining traditional print and bookbinding with augmented reality lets us test how physical books can carry digital layers while staying affordable. The residency funding supports materials and the residency fees; no airfare is requested.

Why ZK/U: it is an artist-initiated venue founded in 2012 in Berlin-Moabit, built around collaborative practice and peer-to-peer learning, and described as a hub for exchange between global discourses and local practice. Our press works the same way: artist-led, community-oriented and driven by materiality and care.
